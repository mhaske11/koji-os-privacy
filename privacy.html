Koji OS

Privacy policy

Last updated 16 October 2026 (covers all connected services)

Who uses Koji OS

Koji OS is a personal dashboard used by one person, its owner, on his own computer. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data it accesses

Only after the owner approves each connection on the provider's own page, Koji OS reads (never writes):

  • Bank and card accounts, through Plaid: balances and transactions, to show spending, card use and net worth. Koji OS cannot move money.
  • Robinhood: accounts, positions, quotes, orders and realized gains, to show the owner's portfolio. Koji OS cannot place, change or cancel orders.
  • Apple iCloud calendars and calendar links: upcoming events, to show the owner's schedule.
  • Google, as below.

What Google data it accesses

With the owner's permission, Koji OS uses these read-only Google permissions:

  • Gmail (read-only): to show the owner his own recent and unread messages on his dashboard.
  • Google Calendar (read-only): to show the owner his own upcoming events on his dashboard.

Koji OS does not send, change or delete email or calendar events.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to display the owner's own information to him.
  • It is stored only on the owner's own computer. Access tokens are stored encrypted; data travels only over encrypted connections.
  • Transactions are kept for up to 2 years and then deleted; disconnecting a service deletes its data and access.
  • It is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Limited Use

Koji OS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect any service from Koji OS at any time, which deletes its stored tokens and data. Google access can also be revoked at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and bank access through my.plaid.com.
