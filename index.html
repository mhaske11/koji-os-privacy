Koji OS

A personal dashboard, built for one person.

Koji OS is a private, single-user dashboard that its owner runs on his own computer. It brings his own information into one place: bank and card balances, investments, calendars and email, goals, bills and projects.

It is not a public service. There are no sign-ups, and it is not offered to anyone else.

When the owner connects his Google account, Koji OS reads (read-only) his Gmail messages and his Google Calendar events, only to show them to him on his own dashboard. It does not send, change or delete anything.
